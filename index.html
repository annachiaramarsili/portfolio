For the "City in August" photography project, the deliberate choice was made to spend a week in Vienna in August 2025 armed only with a Kodak disposable camera limited to 27 exposures. This analog constraint forced a slower, more intentional approach
to capturing the urban landscape. The resulting selection of six images investigates Vienna not through its historical monuments, but as a responsive skin of surfaces transformed by the harsh summer light. The series highlights material behaviors, showcasing how concrete absorbs brightness, glass fractures and redirects light, and water creates dynamic, destabilizing reflections.

Developed at the Politecnico di Milano, "I tre sardi dell'ISIA" is an editorial and conceptual project exploring the design culture of ISIA of Monza through the artistic contributions of three Sardinian designers: Giovanni Pintori, Costantino Nivola, and Salvatore Fancello. The final outcome is a 150x200 mm Research Notebook featuring Bodonian binding that serves as both visual documentation and a hybrid research product. In parallel with the publication, the project included the design of a conceptual meta-exhibition at a 1:50 scale, meant to transform historical research
and editorial decisions into an immersive spatial experience.

"LOOP" is a 3D modeling project developed in collaboration with Rockwell Group, Cork Collective, and Corticeira Amorim to create an innovative cork product for the hospitality sector. The result is a compact cork wine bottle holder designed to seamlessly accommodate the concave indentation, known as the "punt," found at the base of traditional wine bottles. Emphasizing continuity of form and natural sustainability, Loop also features a top surface that catches residual wine drops and discreetly houses two removable cork coasters within its structure for expanded functionality.

Conceived as a comprehensive brand identity project, "GRRILL" draws inspiration from the impetuous figure of Nero to transform the act of barbecuing into a cathartic ritual for venting anger. The brand's visual language relies on the raw, impulsive motifs of charcoal strokes and scribbles, accompanied by the payoff "Stacca la spina, Accendi la griglia" (Unplug. Ignite the grill). The complete visual system encompasses the naming, a dual-version logotype, a brand book, and extensive packaging designs for grills, charcoal, and fire starters. This product ecosystem is further supported by a cohesive print and digital communication campaign.

"Il silenzio delle vittime" (The Silence of the Victims) is a social design campaign aimed at raising awareness about physical gender-based violence by focusing on the unspoken words of victims. The communication system features a triad of 70x100 cm posters where clay was manually spread over wooden letters to visually simulate words struggling to surface, evoking the emotional resistance to speaking out. This physical campaign is complemented by a sixteen-page A3 newspaper that provides deeper context and data, as well as dedicated Instagram and YouTube channels designed to amplify the message in digital spaces.

For the "Notturno marie claire" event held under the dome of the Planetario di Milano in October 2025, this project involved the comprehensive visual and exhibition design of the space. Centered around a starry night illustration by Amy Friend, the refined nocturnal identity was adapted across multiple formats while strictly adhering to the magazine's brand guidelines. The produced graphic materials included the save the date invitation, a customized A5 editorial booklet, spatial graphics for the event carpet, a 2.5x3 meter totem, and a large 6x1.5 meter entrance gate banner.

This professional packaging project involved designing a promotional carrier card for the November 2025 issue of Marie Claire, specifically engineered to hold a Rituals hand cream. The task required creating both the front and back layouts, ensuring a seamless aesthetic balance between the magazine's editorial guidelines and the Rituals brand identity. Careful attention was given to structural and production details, including die-cut dimensions, folding points, and product placement, resulting in
a cohesive and functional packaging solution for the newsstands.
